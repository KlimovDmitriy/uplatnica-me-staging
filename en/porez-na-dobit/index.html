Payment order for corporate profit tax

Profit tax of resident companies. Non-residents use 820-12112-97, withholding tax on dividends 820-12113-94.

Credit: model 18, then the PIB (companies) or JMB (individuals), a dash and the municipality code. Debit: the tax period, e.g. IX/2026.

PIB has 8 digits (companies), JMB 13 (individuals)
