Privacy policy

What you enter

The payment order details (payee, account, amount, reference) stay in your browser. The draft, the print batch and the chosen language are kept only in localStorage on your device. The site sends none of it to a server and has no accounts or database. Clearing the site data in your browser deletes it.

Donations

Donations go directly to the listed payment providers and bank. The site does not process them and keeps no data about donors.
